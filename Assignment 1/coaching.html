What is online coaching?

Online coaching is far more than just training and nutrition plans. What makes my coaching stand out is the accountability, support, EDUCATION and motivation you'll receive along the way. When you have someone checking in weekly, you're far less likely to drift off course. Once you sign up, you'll be assigned a weekly check-in day. Check-ins are done via my coaching app.

Who am I?

My name is Conor Browne. I am 30 year old online coach born and bred in Waterford.

I started training in the gym when I was 17 years old when I started my first semester in college. I had just finished my leaving cert and was very skinny for my age weighing around 50kg, so I decided I wanted to try build some muscle to helo with my confidence.

I fell in love with the gym pretty quickly and I ended up dropping out of my course at the time and pursuing a different degree in Exercise and Health through University of Limerick.

I've been working in the industry for 10 years now where I've worked on the gym floor for 5 of those years across 5 different gyms.

I invested into building my own personal training gym and also started online coaching back in 2018 which was pretty niche at the time.

In 2020, we were hit with the COVID pandameic and this is when my business really took off. I went into full time self employment in 2021 after handing my notice into Goldstone Fitness. In 2022, I moved to Manchester for a year as all my business was now online and I could work from anywhere. I moved home with my partner in 2023 to start a family and save for a house. In 2025 we were blessed with our son, Beau and bought our first home not too long after.

I am now studying BSC in Computing part time in SETU to try secure a more stable job for me and my family's future.

The last 13 years have been a mad journey, but it seems everything has come full circle as I am back where my love for fitness started.

My goals

  1. Finally get my BSC in Computing
  2. Unlock a new career path
  3. Compete in one final bodybuilding show with my son in the crowd
  4. Help more people reach their goals
